# Brand selection: SpotifyCares

I selected **SpotifyCares** from a predeclared shortlist based on outbound-message volume and a manual review of 30 deterministic early-window direct exchanges per candidate. The volume table and fixed review samples are in this directory.

SpotifyCares had 43,265 outbound messages and a 66.2% rough informative-share estimate. More importantly, the review sample contained public, reusable troubleshooting guidance such as restart/log-in steps and information about supported products or requested features. These cases permit a meaningful distinction between a public draft and a necessary escalation.

AmazonHelp had much higher volume, but its reviewed replies frequently sent customers to a generic contact form or private channel. Tesco and Uber_Support also contained many account- or delivery-specific handoffs. Delta included many reservation and confirmation-number cases. Those are valid support interactions, but they give less public guidance for a historical drafting evaluation with no account access.

This is not a claim that Spotify has the best support, nor that historical replies establish current policy. It is a practical choice for testing whether the agent can retrieve and adapt general public guidance while escalating account-specific cases.
